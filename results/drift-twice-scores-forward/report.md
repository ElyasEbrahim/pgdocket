# How should `--twice` read a row's three asks? With jev-1.14 run forward

jev-1.14 was asked twice more on the 300 Kubernetes issues (13 November 2026, $0.026, through the worker as `--twice` does), so its change runs forward here: the stored jev-1.13 answers, then two jev-1.14 asks drawn from its three. Everything else is as in #39, with the same seed. Fixed in issue #40 before the asks.

## The simulation

No API calls. Generated by `jevbench twicescores --forward` (#40) from real asks. Designs, each on the same pairs of re-asks and with the share of the evidence budget #37 chose for rows asked twice:

- **A**: by answers (as now)
- **B**: by scores alone
- **C**: answers and scores, two bets (**chosen**)
- **D**: by answers, else by scores
- **E**: by every option's probability

**The rule, fixed in issue #39 before the first run.** A design is out if its series without a change alarm in more than 5 %, or if its pooled test on #38's six triples of real repeats rejects "the stored one is the odd one a third of the time" at 5 %. Among the rest, the smallest worst-case regret wins (the best design's detection rate minus its own, at the first check after a change and by the last check, in every scenario and setting); within 1 point of it, the smaller mean regret.

**Chosen: C** (answers and scores, two bets). 1,000 series per scenario, 4,000 without a change.
