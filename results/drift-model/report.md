# Can the model check collect evidence that never expires?

No API calls. Generated by `jevbench driftmodel` (#35) from real re-asks: the 300 labelled Kubernetes issues of `k8s_route` and the 2,000 labelled CFPB complaints of Phase 0. Designs:

- **A**: a fixed panel of anchors; the k-th check spends 0.05 · 6 / (π² k²) (#22).
- **B**: the fixed panel, with conformal test martingales on each anchor's asks, on all of the budget.
- **B_0.8**: A on 80 % of the budget, B on the rest.
- **C**: a rotating panel (each check asks labelled rows the series has not asked yet), on A's budget.
- **D_w**: C on a share w of the budget, plus evidence that never expires from each row's first re-ask in the series, on the rest.

**The rule, fixed before the first run.** A design's regret in a scenario is the best design's detection rate there minus its own, in points, at the first check after the change and by the last check. Choose the design whose largest regret is smallest; within 1 point of that, the one with the smaller mean regret.

**Chosen: D_0.7.** 500 runs per scenario, 12 checks each.
