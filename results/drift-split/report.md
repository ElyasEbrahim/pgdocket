# Which share of the error budget should the drift check's window test get?

No API calls. Generated by `jevbench driftsplit` (#34) from the stored Jev answers of `jevbench driftvalidate`.

The rows check of `pgdocket drift` alarms if either of two tests does: the recent window against the rows from before the series began, on a share *w* of the 5 % error budget spent over the checks, and evidence that never expires over every row since, on the rest. #33 used *w* = 0.5. Every share below saw the same draws.

**The rule, fixed before the first run.** A share's regret in a scenario is the best share's detection rate there minus its own, in points. Choose the share whose largest regret is smallest; within 1 point of that, the one with the smaller mean regret. The scenarios are the series (at the first check after the change began, and by the end) and the first checks with injected shifts below. False alarms and the real shifts are reported, not used to choose.

**Chosen: w = 0.8** (in use: 0.8). Self-check: at w = 0.5 and at the share in use, the statistics agreed with `drift.rows_check` on 200 of 200 checks and 200 of 200 checks.
