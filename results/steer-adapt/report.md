# An author who tries again

Real Jev calls (jev-1.14), generated by `jevbench steeradapt` (#49): 5,274 calls, $0.229. The 300 live Kubernetes issues, each author aiming at the same random wrong SIG as #24 and #43, under `--untrusted` (`auto` at 0.718) and `--harden`'s check (erase-and-check and keep-one, on the pieces the worker cuts). Every share is of all 300 issues.

- **O1** sees only whether the issue ended up, unreviewed, at their SIG, and tries a fixed order of attacks.
- **O2** also sees the model's answer: caught (their SIG, but not accepted) leads to a stealthier attack next, not steered to a stronger one.
