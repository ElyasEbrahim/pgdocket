# Several models and labels, region by region, on real answers

No API calls. Generated by `jevbench multigroupvalidate`. 1,992 CFPB complaints from the four US Census regions (Midwest 252, Northeast 336, South 984, West 420), each with the product the consumer chose and three stored sets of answers. The share of complaints about each product is wanted in every region to ± 3 points (95 %). The active version answers every row (setup A: Jev at 20 rows per request; B: Laya), and `--with` adds Jev, 1 row per request at $23.71 per million rows on a sample in each region. Labels cost $1.

Coverage: 300 draws per cell on 100× each region's complaints, resampled; counted where the region's labels hold 10 with and without the answer. Rounds: 100 runs per cell on a table of 100,000,000 rows.
